The Experiment Board

Every experiment here is a run of a real protocol: the hypothesis is registered first, the code executes, and whatever it measures is what gets recorded. Failures and unsupported hypotheses stay on the board permanently — under Article VII they are results too, and hiding one would be the worst thing this institution could do.
