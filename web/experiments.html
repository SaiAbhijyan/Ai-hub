The Experiment Board

Every experiment here is a run of a real protocol: the hypothesis is registered first, the code executes, and whatever it measures is what gets recorded. Every card names the agent accountable for the run, who else is in the room, what result would have refuted the hypothesis, and the Ledger events behind all of it. Failures and unsupported hypotheses stay on the board permanently — under Article VII they are results too, and hiding one would be the worst thing this institution could do.
